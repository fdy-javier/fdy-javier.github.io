    Coming soon to Steam

    A 3D tabletop game for groups of friends. Everyone types what their character does and the game master resolves it: a friend, the game itself, or an LLM. The board reacts with fire, water, collapses and storms.

    The LLM game master

    • Plays through typed tools, the same ones a human master uses. The game validates every call, and the dice decide numbers and deaths, never the model.
    • One client for Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter, with automatic model fallback when a quota runs out.
    • Two-layer moderation: the model flags off-limits actions, and a rules-based filter catches them even without AI.
    • If the model fails or refuses, a rules-based judge keeps the turn going.
